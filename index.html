01 — ABOUT

A bit about
what I do.

I'm a software engineer with around 13 years in the industry. Most of my professional work has been in C# and .NET, across desktop applications, web services, backend systems and software architecture.

A good part of that work has involved connecting software to agricultural machinery and embedded hardware. That means dealing with serial communications, protocols, asynchronous messages and the practical reality that the device on the other end doesn't always behave perfectly.

I enjoy working across the stack, but I'm just as interested in the less visible parts of a system: how it reports a fault, how it recovers when a connection drops, how it gets deployed, and whether the next person can understand it.

Outside work, I tend to apply the same curiosity to small electronics and home projects. They are a good excuse to learn something new and occasionally a reminder that hardware has its own ideas.
