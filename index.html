01 / ABOUT

Curious by nature.
Engineer by trade.

I've spent around 13 years working as a software engineer, mostly in the .NET ecosystem and often in environments where software has to interact with the physical world.

My work has covered software architecture, full-stack development, Windows desktop applications, web services and the less glamorous but important parts of engineering: diagnostics, reliable messaging, deployment and maintaining systems that have been around for a while.

One of the more interesting aspects of my professional experience has been building software that communicates directly with agricultural machinery and embedded hardware. It brings together application development, protocols, serial communications and a healthy respect for what happens when the other end of a connection doesn't behave as expected.

Outside work, I build things involving electronics, sensors, microcontrollers and home automation. I like clear designs, understandable systems and solutions that are maintainable after the original problem has faded from memory.
